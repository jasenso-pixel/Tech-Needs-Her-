Mentor and mentee matching

Pair up with someone a few steps ahead of you, or a few steps behind. Tell us what you want to talk about and we’ll match you each semester.

How it works

You answer four questions. A coordinator pairs you by interest and schedule. You meet about twice a month, over coffee or Zoom, for one semester.
