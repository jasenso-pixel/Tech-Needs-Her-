WHY WE EXIST

She Belongs in Every Room.

Tech Needs Her is a platform created to help female students feel welcomed, represented, and supported in technology. We believe that belonging starts with access to opportunities, meaningful connections, and seeing women who look like you succeed. The future of technology should reflect the people it serves. And that future needs her.
